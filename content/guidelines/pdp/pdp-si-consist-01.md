## What is being checked

Checks whether product data remains internally consistent across titles, attributes, descriptions, and structured elements.

## Why it matters

Contradictory information destroys trust and prevents reliable machine interpretation.

## Failure signals

- Conflicting dimensions
- Different materials listed
- Variant mismatch

## How to verify

1. Cross-check key attributes across sections.

## Recommended fix

Ensure a single authoritative product data source.
