## What is being checked

Whether the PDP communicates real-time availability, delivery timing, or demand signals that encourage timely purchase decisions without misleading tactics.

## Why it matters

Absence of momentum signals reduces purchase immediacy and increases postponement behavior. Clear, factual urgency indicators support faster decision-making and reduce delayed abandonment.

## Failure signals

- • No visible stock availability information
- • No delivery timing information near purchase area
- • No indicators of demand or popularity (if applicable)
- • Urgency signals placed far below primary decision area
- • Use of artificial countdown timers without factual basis
- • Generic urgency messaging not tied to real conditions

## How to verify

1. Inspect area near price and primary CTA.
2. Confirm visibility of stock level or availability status.
3. Verify presence of realistic delivery timeframe messaging.
4. Check for popularity indicators (e.g., bestseller label, review volume, demand signals).
5. Evaluate whether urgency messaging is factual and not artificially inflated.
6. Assess whether signals reinforce decision timing rather than distract.

## Recommended fix

Integrate legitimate urgency and momentum signals near decision-critical areas (price and CTA) to communicate availability, delivery timing, or product demand transparently.
