## What is being checked

Whether the PDP media gallery actively demonstrates product value, use-case relevance, and quality through context-rich visual content.

## Why it matters

Lack of visual proof increases uncertainty and reduces purchase confidence, particularly for first-time visitors. Strong contextual imagery reduces perceived risk, improves perceived quality, and increases add-to-cart likelihood.

## Failure signals

- Product images show only isolated object without contextual usage
- No image demonstrates product in real-life scenario
- No visual detail highlighting material, craftsmanship, or key feature
- Variant differences not visually distinguishable
- Media does not communicate size, scale, or functional outcome
- Video (if present) adds no additional value explanation

## How to verify

1. Review entire product media gallery.
2. Identify whether at least one image shows real-life usage context.
3. Check whether product details (material, texture, mechanism) are visually demonstrated.
4. Evaluate whether image order supports understanding progression.
5. Confirm whether visual content communicates outcome, not just object.
6. Compare variants visually to assess clarity of differentiation.

## Recommended fix

Enhance product media to visually demonstrate use-case, quality, scale, and outcome relevance instead of relying solely on isolated product imagery.
