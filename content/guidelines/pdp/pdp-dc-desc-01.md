## What is being checked

Checks whether the product description explains what the product does and who it is for.

## Why it matters

If the product’s function is unclear, decision confidence collapses and evaluation time increases.

## Failure signals

- Pure marketing language
- Missing use context
- Ambiguous function

## How to verify

1. Review the description and determine whether a first-time visitor can understand the product's purpose without inference.

## Recommended fix

Provide a clear functional description that enables immediate comprehension.
