## What is being checked

Whether the PDP communicates the product’s core benefit and differentiation clearly and immediately within the primary headline and supporting above-the-fold messaging.

## Why it matters

Unclear value propositions reduce conversion by increasing hesitation and forcing users to interpret product relevance themselves. Clear primary value articulation increases confidence, reduces cognitive effort, and accelerates add-to-cart behavior.

## Failure signals

- Product title describes only category or model without benefit context
- No clear articulation of the product’s primary outcome or use-case
- Value proposition requires scrolling to understand
- Messaging focuses on features without communicating user-relevant benefit
- Headline and subheadline do not clarify why this product should be chosen over alternatives

## How to verify

1. Open PDP in clean browser session.
2. Focus on the headline and immediate supporting text above-the-fold.
3. Ask: Can the product’s primary benefit be understood within 5 seconds?
4. Remove brand familiarity assumption. Evaluate from first-time visitor perspective.
5. Document whether the product communicates outcome, differentiation, or only technical description.

## Recommended fix

Rewrite and restructure the primary product messaging so that the core benefit and differentiation of the product are immediately understandable in one concise statement above-the-fold.
