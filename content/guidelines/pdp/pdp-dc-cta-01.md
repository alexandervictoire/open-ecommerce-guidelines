## What is being checked

Whether the primary purchase action remains consistently accessible and contextually reinforced throughout the PDP scroll journey.

## Why it matters

Lack of CTA reinforcement increases decision drop-off after content consumption. Persistent, well-placed CTAs reduce friction and directly increase add-to-cart rate.

## Failure signals

- • Primary CTA only visible above-the-fold
- • No sticky add-to-cart functionality on mobile
- • Long scroll sections without CTA reinforcement
- • Secondary CTAs visually compete with primary action
- • CTA reappears but lacks price or variant context
- • CTA placement inconsistent across devices

## How to verify

1. Scroll entire PDP on desktop and mobile.
2. Identify whether the primary CTA remains accessible after scrolling.
3. Confirm presence of sticky CTA on mobile (if long-form PDP).
4. Evaluate whether repeated CTAs maintain clarity and hierarchy.
5. Check whether price and selected variant remain visible near reinforced CTA.
6. Assess whether CTA reinforcement improves continuity without clutter.

## Recommended fix

Ensure that the primary add-to-cart action is consistently accessible throughout the decision journey via strategic CTA repetition or sticky behavior without creating visual clutter.
